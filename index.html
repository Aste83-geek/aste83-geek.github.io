How it works

01

Submit

Developers nominate their own PICO-8 games.

02

Selection

Games are individually reviewed for a curated collection. Submission does not guarantee selection.

03

Design & approval

Selected developers review the card design and separately approve its permitted uses.

04

Discover

Approved cards appear in the gallery, linking back to their original games.

For PICO-8 creators

Put your game on the map.

Want your game to be considered for a collectible card? Tell us about it! We review each submission and retain editorial control over the collection.

Applying does not grant us permission to print or sell your game's artwork. If selected, we'll request a separate, explicit rights authorization and send the final card for your approval.

This prototype does not send or store submissions. You can copy the application and send it manually, or connect a form service when launching.

About the project

Built for the love of tiny games.

PICO-8 Game Cards is an independent fan initiative inspired by vintage video game packaging, retro trading cards and the creativity of the PICO-8 community. Each card is a small tribute to its creator, with a link to discover the original game.

Unofficial project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Lexaloffle or PICO-8. Game artwork remains the property of its respective rights holders. Public release and any paid printing require the relevant permissions. Add your legal notices and privacy policy before collecting real submissions.

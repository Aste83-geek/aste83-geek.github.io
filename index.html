Game submission

Card permissions (all required)

To submit your game, check all four boxes. If we select it, we’ll ask you to confirm these permissions in writing after you approve the final front and back.

The proposed permission is non-exclusive and worldwide for three years from the date of your written confirmation, with no limit on the number of copies printed during that period. For six months after normal expiry, existing stock may be sold or given away as authorized.

FAQ

Can I submit a game under any license?

Yes. PICO-8 games are welcome regardless of their license, including Creative Commons and all-rights-reserved games. If your game is selected, we’ll ask the relevant rights holders to confirm the permissions for the final card.

What happens after I submit my game?

I’ll email you my decision as soon as I can. I run this project in my spare time, so thank you for your patience if it takes me a little while to reply. If your game is selected, I’ll send you the front and back of your card for review. Once you approve the design, we’ll ask you to confirm all four permissions in writing.

Can I take part with an online-only card?

Every submission must include permission for online publication, printing, free distribution and sale. The gallery previews a collection intended for print, and the project’s goal is to put physical cards in players’ hands.

When will physical cards be available?

We aim to launch the first print run once the gallery reaches 50 approved cards.

Can the organizer remove an approved card?

Until the first print run begins, the organizer reserves the right to remove any card from the collection and take it down from the online gallery without giving a reason, even after the card has been approved and published.

Can I remove my card or change my permissions?

If you own the rights to the game, you can email pico-8-gamecards@gmail.com at any time to request that your card be removed or that its permissions be changed.

Is this project supported by Lexaloffle Games or PICO-8?

PICO-8 Game Cards is an independent fan project. I’ve shared it with people involved with Lexaloffle Games and PICO-8. The project has no official affiliation, endorsement or sponsorship, and neither Lexaloffle Games nor the PICO-8 team is involved in running it.

About the project

Built for the love of tiny games.

PICO-8 Game Cards is an independent fan initiative inspired by vintage video game packaging, retro trading cards and the creativity of the PICO-8 community. Each card is a small tribute to its creator, with a link to discover the original game.

Unofficial project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Lexaloffle or PICO-8. Game artwork remains the property of its respective rights holders. We use approved artwork only for the purposes agreed in writing.
